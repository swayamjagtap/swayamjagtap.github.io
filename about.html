Who I am

I’m Swayam, a third-year Data Science student based in Mumbai. Academically speaking, you’ll find me trying to decipher my own code or figuring out how to actually put my degree to use in the real world.

When I finally force myself to step away from the laptop, I’m usually out for a jog or a walk, getting a calisthenics workout in (who needs a gym, anyway?), or playing video games with a good playlist on repeat. I'm also a highly social person who loves getting out, meeting new people, and making friends. And on race weekends? You'll find me glued to the screen watching F1, hoping for a Max Verstappen masterclass.
