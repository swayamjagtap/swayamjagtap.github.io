The First Week of Chaos and Code

July 25, 2026

Before we start, I want to say this to anyone expecting a traditional, formal blog: you’re in the wrong place. Since this is my very first post—where I’ll just be introducing myself and talking about my first week—I've decided to keep things light.

For those who don't know me, I'm Swayam (which actually translates to "self" in Sanskrit). Academically, I'm a Data Science student who woke up one day and decided, "Hey! Let’s make a website." The goal is to have a space where I can publish my work and projects—whether they're educational, silly, or completely meaningless-yet-fun. I figured while I’m at it, I might as well write weekly blogs to talk about what I’ve been doing, hold myself accountable for my targets, and most importantly, just be myself.

This past week was absolute chaos! I had my cricket, football, and rugby tournaments. (Don’t ask me how it went, because we lost in the quarter-finals.) Between all that rushing around, I still had to keep up with my Python lectures. Honestly, I had zero energy left to start something totally new, so I decided to revise and refine my previous projects instead.

Yes, that might sound like a convenient excuse for not progressing further, but I feel there’s a silver lining here. There is a huge difference between just completing a course and actually learning a new skill. Anyone can finish a course on time, but implementing what you've learned? That's not everyone’s cup of tea.
